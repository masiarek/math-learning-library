# Group: associative, with an identity and an inverse for every element

**Level:** 101 · one row of the table on [the laws of an operation](../../README.md), on its own page

**One line:** A group is a monoid in which every element can be undone, so every equation a · x = b has exactly one solution x = a⁻¹ · b; ℤ under +, nonzero fractions under ×, and the six shufflings of three objects are groups, and ℕ under + is not, because nothing undoes 3.

## Laws required

associative, identity, inverses, of the four on [the parent page](../../README.md#the-names-are-only-combinations).

## What it is, and the examples

A **group** is a monoid in which every x has an **inverse**, a y with x · y = e = y · x. Simovici and Djeraba's Definition 1.143 writes it as an algebra of type (0, 2, 1): the constant e, the operation, and the unary operation x ↦ x⁻¹. The inverse is unique, by the one-line argument y = y · e = y · (x · y′) = (y · x) · y′ = e · y′ = y′.

The examples in the program: ℤ under + with inverse −n; the nonzero fractions under × with inverse 1/q; and the six permutations of three objects under composition, the smallest group that is not commutative, which the program shows by finding two shuffles that do not commute. The non-examples are ℕ under +, a monoid in which 3 has no inverse, and ℤ under ×, in which 2 has none: an inverse must exist *inside the set*, which is why "which elements can be divided by" is the question [multiplication can be undone](../../../../03_Complex_Numbers/multiplication_can_be_undone/README.md) asks.

What inverses buy is solving: in a group, a · x = b has exactly one solution, x = a⁻¹ · b, so every element appears exactly once in every row and column of the operation table, a Latin square. That is the structure behind cancelling in equations, behind every symmetry (the symmetries of any object form a group under composition), and behind the arithmetic of ℤ/mℤ that [equivalence relations](../../../../04_Sets/equivalence_and_partitions/README.md) builds.

## Po polsku, w skrócie

Grupa to monoid, w którym każdy element ma odwrotność: x · y = e = y · x. Przykłady: ℤ z + (odwrotność −n), ułamki niezerowe z × (odwrotność 1/q), sześć permutacji trzech obiektów ze składaniem, najmniejsza grupa nieprzemienna. Kontrprzykłady: ℕ z + (3 nie ma odwrotności), ℤ z × (2 nie ma). Odwrotności dają rozwiązywanie równań: a · x = b ma dokładnie jedno rozwiązanie x = a⁻¹ · b, a tabela działania jest kwadratem łacińskim.

## Auf Deutsch: Stichwörter

Gruppe: assoziativ, mit neutralem Element und einem Inversen für jedes Element.

**Stichwörter:** Gruppe, neutrales Element, Inverses, Assoziativgesetz, Gruppentafel, lateinisches Quadrat.
