# Monoid: associative, with an identity element

**Level:** 101 · one row of the table on [the laws of an operation](../../README.md), on its own page

**One line:** A monoid is a semigroup with a do-nothing element: ℕ under × with 1, ℕ under gcd with 0, strings under concatenation with the empty string, sets under ∪ with ∅; it is the structure in which an empty chain has a value.

## Laws required

associative, identity, of the four on [the parent page](../../README.md#the-names-are-only-combinations).

## What it is, and the examples

A **monoid** is a set with an associative operation and an **identity**, an element e with e · x = x = x · e for every x. Simovici and Djeraba's Definition 1.141 writes it as an algebra of type (0, 2): one zero-ary operation, the constant e, and one binary operation. The identity is unique, since if e and e′ both are identities then e = e · e′ = e′.

The examples in the program: ℕ under × with identity 1; ℕ under gcd with identity 0, since gcd(0, n) = n, the example Simovici and Djeraba give and prove associative; strings under concatenation with the empty string, the monoid every programming language has; the subsets of a set under ∪ with ∅ and under ∩ with U, which are the [identity laws](../../../../04_Sets/algebra_of_sets/laws/identity/README.md) of set algebra; and ℕ under + with 0. The non-example is the positive integers under +, a semigroup that is not a monoid because 0 is missing.

What the identity buys is a value for the empty chain: the sum of no numbers is 0, the product of no numbers is 1, the concatenation of no strings is "", the union of no sets is ∅. That is why `sum([])` is 0, why `math.prod([])` is 1, and why a fold needs an initial value exactly when the list may be empty.

## Po polsku, w skrócie

Monoid to półgrupa z elementem neutralnym e, takim że e · x = x = x · e. Przykłady: ℕ z mnożeniem i 1, ℕ z gcd i 0 (bo gcd(0, n) = n), napisy ze sklejaniem i pustym napisem, podzbiory z ∪ i ∅. Element neutralny daje wartość pustemu łańcuchowi: suma niczego to 0, iloczyn niczego to 1, `sum([])` to 0. Dodatnie liczby całkowite z + to półgrupa, ale nie monoid, bo brakuje 0.

## Auf Deutsch: Stichwörter

Monoid: assoziativ, mit neutralem Element, aber nicht jedes Element hat ein Inverses.

**Stichwörter:** Monoid, neutrales Element, Zeichenketten unter Verkettung, natürliche Zahlen unter Addition.
