On the real number line, how is a point located?	By one real number, called the coordinate of the point.	definition
How many numbers locate a point in a plane, and what are they called together?	Two. Together they form an ordered pair (x, y), the coordinates of the point.	definition
In a rectangular coordinate system, what is the horizontal number line called?	The x-axis.	definition
In a rectangular coordinate system, what is the vertical number line called?	The y-axis.	definition
What is the origin O?	The point where the x-axis and the y-axis intersect. It reads 0 on both axes, so its coordinates are (0, 0).	definition
Must the x-axis and the y-axis use the same scale?	No. Mathematics usually uses the same scale on both; an application may use a different scale on each axis.	definition
Which points on the x-axis carry positive numbers, and which carry negative numbers?	Points to the right of O are positive; points to the left of O are negative.	definition
Which points on the y-axis carry positive numbers, and which carry negative numbers?	Points above O are positive; points below O are negative.	definition
What does the arrowhead at the end of an axis mean?	It marks the positive direction of that axis.	definition
What are the two names of the coordinate system built from two perpendicular number lines?	The rectangular coordinate system, also called the Cartesian coordinate system, after René Descartes.	definition
What is the xy-plane?	The plane in which the x-axis and the y-axis lie.	definition
What are the coordinate axes?	The x-axis and the y-axis.	definition
In the ordered pair (x, y) of a point P, what does x measure?	The signed distance of P from the y-axis: how far right or left P is.	signed-distance
In the ordered pair (x, y) of a point P, what does y measure?	The signed distance of P from the x-axis: how far up or down P is.	signed-distance
What does signed mean in the signed distance x?	x > 0 when P is to the right of the y-axis, and x < 0 when P is to the left of it.	signed-distance
The x-coordinate measures distance from which axis, the x-axis or the y-axis?	The y-axis. It measures right or left of the vertical line, and the vertical line is the y-axis.	signed-distance
What is the ordered pair (x, y) of a point P called?	The coordinates of P.	definition
How do you plot the point (−3, 1)?	Go 3 units along the x-axis to the left of O, then straight up 1 unit, and place a dot there.	plotting
What does it mean to plot a point?	To place a dot at the location in the plane given by its coordinates.	plotting
What are the coordinates of the origin?	(0, 0).	definition
What form do the coordinates of a point on the x-axis have?	(x, 0): the y-coordinate is 0.	axes
What form do the coordinates of a point on the y-axis have?	(0, y): the x-coordinate is 0.	axes
A point has y-coordinate 0. Where is it?	On the x-axis.	axes
A point has x-coordinate 0. Where is it?	On the y-axis.	axes
What is another name for the x-coordinate of a point?	The abscissa.	vocabulary
What is another name for the y-coordinate of a point?	The ordinate.	vocabulary
What is the abscissa of a point?	Its x-coordinate.	vocabulary
What is the ordinate of a point?	Its y-coordinate.	vocabulary
How is the point P whose coordinates are (x, y) usually written and said?	Written P = (x, y), and said simply the point (x, y).	vocabulary
What are the quadrants?	The four regions into which the coordinate axes divide the xy-plane, numbered I to IV counterclockwise starting from the upper right.	quadrants
Signs of x and y in quadrant I?	x > 0 and y > 0 (upper right).	quadrants
Signs of x and y in quadrant II?	x < 0 and y > 0 (upper left).	quadrants
Signs of x and y in quadrant III?	x < 0 and y < 0 (lower left).	quadrants
Signs of x and y in quadrant IV?	x > 0 and y < 0 (lower right).	quadrants
A point has x < 0 and y > 0. Which quadrant?	Quadrant II.	quadrants
A point has x > 0 and y < 0. Which quadrant?	Quadrant IV.	quadrants
A point has x < 0 and y < 0. Which quadrant?	Quadrant III.	quadrants
A point has x > 0 and y > 0. Which quadrant?	Quadrant I.	quadrants
Which quadrant is a point on a coordinate axis in?	None. Points on the axes belong to no quadrant, because 0 is neither positive nor negative.	quadrants
Which quadrant is (3, −2) in?	Quadrant IV: x > 0 and y < 0.	quadrants
Which quadrant is (−2, −3) in?	Quadrant III: x < 0 and y < 0.	quadrants
Which quadrant is (−3, 1) in?	Quadrant II: x < 0 and y > 0.	quadrants
Which quadrant is (3, 2) in?	Quadrant I: x > 0 and y > 0.	quadrants
In which order are the quadrants numbered around the origin?	Counterclockwise: I upper right, II upper left, III lower left, IV lower right.	quadrants
Are (2, 5) and (5, 2) the same point?	No. An ordered pair remembers which entry is first: (2, 5) is 2 right and 5 up; (5, 2) is 5 right and 2 up.	ordered-pair
Why is a quadrant nothing but a pair of signs?	Membership depends only on whether x and y are positive or negative, never on their sizes: (1, 1) and (1000, 5) are both in quadrant I.	reasoning
What happens to the quadrant of (x, y) when x is replaced by −x?	The point reflects across the y-axis: quadrants I and II swap, and III and IV swap.	reasoning
In which quadrants is the product xy positive?	I and III, where x and y have the same sign. It is negative in II and IV.	reasoning
